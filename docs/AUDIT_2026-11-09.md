# Portfolio and XR Works curation audit — 2026-11-09

## Scope and limitations

Reviewed ewanqian/portfolio Home, Works, Writing, Archive and Applications source, plus ewanqian/performable-music hand-osc-lab files and Greenhouse-in-Transit project/experience documentation. The Applications source contains **18 records**, but its public filter displays **2** entries. This is a source and UX audit, not measured site analytics or a Quest hardware evaluation.

## Portfolio's four actual purposes

1. **Artist's authorship** — coherent, publicly exhibited audiovisual and spatial works with professional photos/video, collaborators, artistic statement, precise site and credit.
2. **Delivery confidence** — selected screen, scanning, realtime, performance and installation cases with roles, constraints, on-site proof and outcomes.
3. **Research authority** — complete Writing articles (BPM System 2026 etc.), with reproducible methods, figures, experiments and differentiated theory/practice.
4. **Actionable demo gateway** — two known public hand-input demo links plus an entry into XR Works. It should not become a list of all prototype variants.

Untapped strategic value: connect a flagship Work → technical Writing → one verified runnable experience → workshop/production proposal. Completion, quality and proof of practice matter more than experiment count.

## What the source gets right

- Home now has an editorial hero, selected projects, research notes and clear work navigation.
- Works, Production, Writing, Archive and Apps have separate roles.
- Writing only treats content with an actual article and published status as published.
- Apps now shows only Hand / Space / OSC and Hand / Synth / Space V2 while keeping historical source entries.
- Greenhouse has its own story and repository rather than an arbitrary gesture toy.

## Where it creates professional risk

- Unfinished tests and gameplay-inspired experiments listed as equivalent Work entries devalue curated pieces.
- All-in-one Performable Music currently mixes live music research with the entire WebXR input testbench.
- Duplicate magic, Warlock, neural and symbolic-input variants consume QA and maintenance while not guaranteeing a distinctive artistic outcome.
- Static manually updated application timestamps can get out of sync.
- The checked-in generated writings.js is stale, but production React builds invoke react/package.json prebuild → scripts/build-content.js, so published writing is regenerated. Do not infer a live outage from source-only generated files. The root CI explicitly asserts BPM System, ENTROPY and Performable Music catalog entries after build.
- Browser smoke tests must not be described as actual Quest/AVP acceptance.
- The Greenhouse proposal is ahead of its final rendered 3D assets and physical-case MR registration.
- Credit and team collaboration need persistent provenance on every completed work.

## A lean taxonomy, not more homepage navigation

**Portfolio**
- Selected Works (artistic authorship)
- Production / Services (verified commissions)
- Writing (method and cultural research)
- About/CV and Archive
- Apps as a thin link gateway

**XR Works internal tracks only**
- Input & Runtime
- Interaction Studies
- Experience Prototypes
- Selected XR Works
- Research Notes

**Performable Music**
- compositions / score grammar / arrangement
- live AV instruments and synchronization
- performance demos with true musical purpose

**Greenhouse in Transit**
- narrative, original props, web archive, installation assets, VR/MR story, operator docs

Avoid new first-level Portfolio nav categories.

## Priority and progress standard

Maintain **at most two active XR production experiences** at once: Greenhouse + one shared interaction system. Archive/research others without building 15 parallel unfinished public-facing products.

Prototype becomes a selected artwork only if it has a distinctive artistic question, a complete beginning-middle-end experience, evidence video, controlled visual/audio quality, credits and real-device QA.

Best latent business uses: a reusable live AV control demo for venue/commission pitch; workshop materials derived from real hand→OSC→sound tests; and concise evidence-backed technical writing. No claim of audience demand or financial ROI without analytics.
